# Sahana & Kevin – Luxury Digital Wedding Invitation

A digital wedding invitation web application crafted for **Sahana & Kevin**, designed with a **clean luxury white page background**, **rich deep wine stationery cards**, and **champagne-gold typography & borders**, featuring their official golden monogram crest.

---

## 🌹 Design Palette & Aesthetics

- **Page Background**: Clean Luxury White / Soft Ivory (`#FAF8F5`, `#FFFFFF`).
- **Stationery Cards**: Deep Wine & Burgundy (`#50132D`, `#3B0A20`, `#250514`) with 1.5px Champagne-Gold borders and ornate filigree corners.
- **Card Typography**:
  - Headings & Accents: Champagne Gold (`#D4AF62`) & Metallic Gold (`#C5A04D`)
  - Couple Names Calligraphy: *Alex Brush* / *Great Vibes* with metallic gold shimmer animation
  - Subheadings & Labels: Soft Gold (`#E8D39B`)
  - Body Text: Warm Ivory (`#FFF7E8`)
- **Outer Headings**: Deep Wine (`#4A1028`) and Royal Wine (`#781E42`).
- **Official Monogram Crest**: Official circular **S&K** golden laurel crest featured on the envelope seal, navbar, cover crest, family blessings emblem, and final closing section.

---

## ✨ Sections & Layout

1. **3D Animated Luxury Portrait Envelope**:
   - Vertical envelope with embossed golden rose damask patterns on the flaps.
   - Official S&K golden wax seal with soft breathing glow.
   - Upper flap unfolds 180° upward in 3D, emitting a warm golden light flare before smoothly dissolving into the main invitation cover.
   - Includes *Skip to Invitation* button and audio auto-start on tap.

2. **Main Wedding Invitation Cover**:
   - Official S&K crest, double gold borders, ornate gold corners.

4. **Family Details**:

   - **Bride**: Daughter of Triveni Bhaskar & (Late) Balappa Bhaskar (Banaswadi, Bangalore).

5. **Wedding Countdown & Calendar**:
   - Live Days : Hours : Minutes : Seconds timer calibrated to **Asia/Kolkata (IST)**.
   - *Add to Google Calendar* and *Download Apple / Outlook iCal (.ics)* buttons.

6. **Venue, Schedule & Google Maps**:
   - **Eden Farms**, Dasanayakanahalli, Hennur-Bagalur Road, Bangalore, Karnataka.
